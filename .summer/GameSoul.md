Vision

You're creating an escape room game set inside a microwave. It's a bold, unexpected mashup that turns a mundane kitchen appliance into a puzzle-filled world where players must solve their way out before time runs out.

Genre and Inspirations

This is an escape room game in the purest sense: logic puzzles, hidden clues, environmental exploration, and the pressure of time. The player's goal is singular and clear: unlock the mystery and escape. You're drawing on the psychological tension and "aha!" moment satisfaction that makes escape room games so addictive.

Look and Feel

Your game lives in a retro tech aesthetic: think glowing buttons, chunky interfaces, maybe a hint of 80s or 90s industrial design. The inside of the microwave could feel like a control room or a strange technical space. Muted colors (grays, blacks, pale greens or reds from LED displays) mixed with bright accent colors for interactive elements. It should feel functional but slightly uncanny, like you've stepped inside a machine.

Core Mechanics

Players explore the microwave's interior, examining objects and surfaces, discovering clues hidden in unexpected places. They interact with buttons, dials, digital displays, and physical objects to solve puzzles. Each puzzle solved brings them closer to opening the door. The core loop is: observe, hypothesize, interact, solve, repeat.

What Makes It Special

The genius of your idea is the constraint itself. A microwave is a small, specific space with its own logic and aesthetics. That limitation forces creative puzzle design and gives the game instant identity and charm. Players know exactly where they are, which makes the puzzle space feel manageable yet surprising. It's quirky, memorable, and absolutely buildable.
